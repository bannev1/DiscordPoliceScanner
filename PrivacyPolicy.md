# Privacy Policy for Local Police Scanner

**Last Updated:** October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Local Police Scanner** ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Bot on Discord.

By adding the Bot to your server or interacting with it, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To function properly and provide localized police scanner feeds, the Bot collects and stores minimal data:

### A. Location Data
* **User-Set Location Preferences:** When you or a server administrator configure location settings (such as city, county, ZIP code, or state), we store these preferences to filter and route relevant scanner feeds to your channel.
* **No Real-Time Tracking:** We **do not** collect, track, or request exact background GPS location data. All location settings are explicitly provided by users through commands.

### B. Discord Identifiers
* **Server (Guild) IDs & Channel IDs:** Stored to remember where scanner alerts and feeds should be sent.
* **User IDs:** Stored when you save personal preferences, set default feeds, or execute configuration commands.
* **Command Usage Data:** Basic metadata regarding bot commands (e.g., timestamp, command executed) to monitor performance and debug errors.

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
* Message content outside of commands addressed directly to the Bot.
* Personal identifiable information (PII) such as full names, email addresses, or phone numbers (unless voluntarily provided for support).
* Voice or audio recordings from Discord channels.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
* **Service Delivery:** To fetch and display local scanner feeds, audio transmissions, and alerts relevant to your chosen location.
* **Configuration Persistence:** To ensure your server's settings and feed preferences remain saved across bot updates and restarts.
* **Bot Maintenance & Security:** To analyze command logs, troubleshoot bugs, and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Storage

* Configuration data (Server IDs, Channel IDs, set locations) is retained for as long as the Bot remains in your server or until you manually clear your settings.
* Temporary command logs and diagnostic data are automatically purged on a regular basis.
* If the Bot is removed from a server, stored settings associated with that server may be deleted during periodic database cleanups.

---

## 4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information or server data to third parties. 

* **Public Scanner Feeds:** The Bot aggregates publicly broadcasted radio feeds and data from third-party sources. No user data is sent to these third-party audio streams.
* **Discord Platform:** Data transmitted through the Bot is subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Control

You have full control over the data stored by Local Police Scanner:
* **Modifying Location Settings:** You can update or overwrite your saved location preferences at any time using the Bot's configuration commands.
* **Data Deletion:** Server administrators can remove the Bot from their server to discontinue data collection. You may also contact us to request the complete deletion of your server or user configuration data from our database.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our features or legal requirements. Updated versions will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

---

## 7. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official support server or developer contact details listed in the Bot's `/help` or `/info` command.
